Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how Connections Grouping Assistant works, our linguistic analysis methodology, and data privacy.

How does the semantic API work?

When you input 16 words, our system queries the Datamuse linguistic engine. The algorithm executes pairwise comparisons across all inputs, assessing semantic co-occurrence, shared dictionary definitions, and latent categorical linkages.

What is the Quick Paste feature?

Quick Paste parses unstructured text strings pasted directly from clipboards. It automatically strips out commas, spaces, or line breaks to populate all 16 input grid slots simultaneously without requiring manual entry.

Is this tool affiliated with the New York Times?

No. Connections Grouping Assistant is an entirely independent linguistic utility designed for educational analysis and word puzzle assistance. It holds no affiliation, endorsement, or sponsorship with The New York Times Company.

Are input words stored on your servers?

No. Words submitted into the grouping tool are processed dynamically in real-time within your web browser session and are never recorded or stored in persistent databases.
