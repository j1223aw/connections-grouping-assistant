Helpful Answers to Common Questions

🧩 How does this helper organize the 16 words?

When you enter your 16 words, our helper compares each word against all the others on the board. It looks for four distinct groups: words with the same dictionary meaning (Yellow), words sharing a hobby or theme (Green), general knowledge or compound words (Blue), and spelling or letter tricks (Purple). It makes sure every single word has its own rightful place.

🛡️ What is the "Spoiler Shield" and how does it protect your fun?

Nothing ruins a word game faster than accidentally seeing all the answers! That's why our helper uses a gentle, 3-step hint system:

1. Category Theme Nudge: Gives you a gentle clue about the group theme without revealing which words belong to it.
2. Starter Words: Reveals just two words from the group, leaving you the fun of finding the other two on your own.
3. Full Solution Reveal: Shows the complete 4-word answer only when you click to reveal it.

⚠️ What is a "Red Herring" and how do you warn about them?

A red herring is a decoy word designed to distract you. For example, you might see 5 words that all seem to be types of birds, but one actually belongs to a completely different group. When our helper identifies 5 words that fit one theme, it flags the overlap so you don't waste one of your 4 guesses.

🚫 Is using this helper considered cheating?

Not at all. Think of it like working through a puzzle with a partner at the table. Rather than giving away answers, it offers progressive clues so you can work through the logic yourself, discover the connections, and keep your daily streak alive.

🔒 What data does this website collect?

Your privacy is completely protected. Your daily streak is saved right inside your own browser on your phone or computer—we never send it to any outside server. The words you enter are only used to find hints and are never saved or sold. This site is completely free and safe to use. Review our Privacy Policy for full details.
