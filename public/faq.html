Engine Architecture & Hint Philosophy

🧩 How does the neural solver partition the 16-word grid?

When you submit a 16-word grid, our backend executes a secure Cloud Run function powered by Gemini. Rather than performing simple dictionary lookups, the model evaluates the combinatorial space of all $\binom{16}{4} = 1,820$ possible 4-word groupings simultaneously.

It cross-references semantic clusters, slang, parts of speech, compound prefixes/suffixes, and phonetics. Once candidates are assembled, our deterministic watchdog verifies that exactly four disjoint subsets of four words are constructed with zero duplication before sending the payload to your browser.

🛡️ What is the "Spoiler Shield" and how does it protect streaks?

Standard word solvers ruin the fun by immediately dumping the four completed answer groups. The Connections Grouping Assistant was engineered with a 3-tier progressive hint disclosure model:

1. Linguistic Category Nudge: Reveals a masked summary of the category rule (e.g., Words Ending in 'BOARD') without revealing which tiles belong to it.
2. Anchor Clues: Highlights exactly two anchor words that anchor the grouping, leaving you to deduce the remaining two candidates independently.
3. Full Solution Reveal: Unlocks the complete tier only when you explicitly confirm you are ready.

⚠️ How do you identify and warn about Red Herrings?

A red herring is created when five or six words on a board share an overt contextual feature (e.g., five flying animals or five colors). Our backend runs an association graph across all sixteen words to detect when a single theme claims more than four tiles. When detected, the assistant flags a Red Herring Advisory alerting you that at least one candidate is a double-agent belonging to a harder tier.

🚫 Is using this tool considered cheating?

We built this tool as an educational companion, deductive trainer, and streak-preservation safety net. By providing staged nudges and anchor words rather than raw solutions, players learn to spot lateral wordplay mechanisms and homophone transformations that transfer directly to future solving sessions.

🔒 What data does this tool collect?

We prioritize minimal data collection. Your daily solver streaks are saved locally in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to external servers. Word submissions are processed transiently in server memory to generate hints and are never sold or used to build user profiles. Review our Privacy Policy for full compliance details.
