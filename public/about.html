ABOUT CONNECTIONS ASSISTANT

Helping Puzzle Lovers Protect Their Streaks Without Spoilers

Connections Grouping Assistant (created and maintained by ConnectionsCoach) was built for a simple frustration every daily word-game player knows: seeing a clever four-word trap, wanting to test your logic on paper before risking one of your four precious New York Times guesses, and not wanting an answer key to accidentally spoil the fun.

Our Core Philosophy

🛡️ Zero-Spoiler Practice First Our main scratchboard loads today's 16 words in randomized order so you can group, copy, paste, and experiment safely without seeing a single category name or color tier.
💡 Gentle, Progressive Nudges Instead of dumping all 4 answers immediately, our hint engine and daily Reddit dispatches give you nudge-by-nudge clues—from masked themes to decoy warnings.
📖 Deep Word Meanings & Origins We believe puzzle solving is a daily brain workout. Every day we curate etymologies, usage examples, and wordplay mechanics for all 16 words so solvers learn why a connection works.
🎬 Visual Daily Walkthroughs On our Breakdowns page and YouTube channel (@ConnectionsAssistant), we publish quick 45-second decoy alerts and full 16:9 deep dives every morning.

How We Curate Daily Puzzles

Every night after the official puzzle drops, we analyze the board's semantic structure across the four cognitive difficulty tiers:

  • Yellow (Direct Synonyms): Words sharing an immediate, literal definition or membership.
  • Green (Contextual Link): Words united by a real-world theme, activity, or setting.
  • Blue (Trivia & Compound Patterns): Cultural references, sets, or structural phrases.
  • Purple (Lateral Wordplay): Hidden prefixes/suffixes, homophones, letter swaps, or inside-the-word tricks.

We specifically identify the day's Red Herring / Decoy Trap—the intentional overlap designed to bait players into burning their first guess—and build our hints around helping you spot that trap early.
